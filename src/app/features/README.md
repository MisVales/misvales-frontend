# Features

Etapa 1 established the feature template. Access and organization are implemented. Etapa 7 adds
fail-closed `points` and `risk` boundaries, and Etapa 9 adds the equivalent `reporting`,
`notifications`, and `audit` boundaries. Their HTTP clients cover the published routes, but their
pages do not render inferred business data while the upstream Resources remain undefined. See
`docs/stage-7-status.md` and `docs/stage-9-status.md`.
